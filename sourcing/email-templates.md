# Outreach templates — Japan MTM supplier vetting

Rules (from the sourcing plan): bilingual, polite business Japanese first, English second.
**Never mention target prices** — let the supplier name numbers first. Personalize the
first paragraph per company. One follow-up after 7–10 days of silence, then stop.
