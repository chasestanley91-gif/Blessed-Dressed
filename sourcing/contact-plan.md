## When replies arrive

Forward the reply (most will be Japanese) — it will be translated and a response drafted.
Ask in this order, and let **them** name prices first:
1. Made-in-Japan routing confirmed in writing (SADA→Miyagi; Yamaki; Sample Koubou)
2. Sample garment: process, cost, lead time
3. Pattern intake format (CAD/CAM data, paper pattern, fit garment) + posture-adjustment range
4. Remake/alteration policy when a garment misses spec
5. Brand label sew-in + packaging; US shipping method and who pays duty
6. Wholesale price structure at 1 / 10 / 50 garments per month, and rush options

## Follow-up cadence

- Day 7–10 after silence: one polite bilingual nudge (template in email-templates.md), then stop.
- Track status in `outreach-log.json` (created at execution).
