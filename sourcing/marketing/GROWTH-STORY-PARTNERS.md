# Blessed & Dressed — Growth Story

*For partners, suppliers, and collaborators. September 2026.*

## The opportunity

Blessed & Dressed is a made-to-measure menswear house built around a positioning nobody else in
the category owns: **Christian bespoke suiting** — craftsmanship and faith identity in the same
product, based in Alexandria, Louisiana. The brand voice, the configurator, and the sourcing
relationships are already built. What's ahead is scaling demand to match the production capacity
now coming online.

## Where things stand today

- A working made-to-measure builder — an 8-step configurator with a real body/finished
  measurement system — plus product, collection, and fabric-book pages, all live.
- Two named seasonal collections in market: Summer Tropical Wools and Winter Atelier.
- A production partnership with **Tailorcloth S.C.** (Poland/Spain) ramping toward standing
  volume, alongside a second production track in active negotiation in Japan.
- A fabric sourcing network of **60+ mills across 13 countries** — from verified sub-$20/yd
  domestic wool to named European bunches (Dormeuil, Vitale Barberis Canonico, Holland & Sherry,
  Dugdale) — giving the business real range from an accessible entry price to a true bespoke
  offer.
- Zero paid marketing spend to date. No social presence, no blog, no email list yet — which
  means the growth plan below starts from a clean slate, not a channel that needs fixing.

## The targets

| | Balance of 2026 | 2027 |
|---|---|---|
| Shirts | 150 | 450 |
| Suits | 75 | 225 |

The 2027 number isn't a further escalation — it's the same weekly pace as the 2026 push, held
steady across a full year instead of four months. That's a repeatability problem, not a
scale-up problem, and the plan below is built around solving it.

## Go-to-market: three pillars, capital-efficient by design

1. **Community-led guerrilla marketing** — church and ministry-leader partnerships, local trunk
   shows, referral programs with photographers and wedding venues. Louisiana-rooted, high-trust,
   near-zero cost.
2. **Content-led SEO** — the fabric-sourcing and craftsmanship research already on file becomes
   customer-facing content: mill stories, fit education, the faith-forward brand story. Built to
   compound rather than to be re-bought every month like paid media.
3. **Organic social and referral** — before/after customer content, founder story, and a
   referral-credit program that pays for itself only when it converts.

No pillar depends on ad spend to function. Paid acquisition is a layer to add later, once organic
and community channels have shown which message converts — not a dependency to get started.

## Why this works

- **A real market gap.** Faith-forward positioning in menswear is underserved, and it comes with
  a built-in, low-cost-of-acquisition distribution channel: churches and ministry networks refer
  by trust, not by ad spend.
- **A margin structure that supports it.** Fabric is billed to the customer as a transparent,
  cost-plus line rather than absorbed into the garment price — so the business can offer a real
  entry-level price point without giving up the option to sell a true named-bunch bespoke suit to
  the customer who wants one.
- **Production and demand are being built in parallel, not sequentially** — the sourcing and
  production relationships already exist; this plan is what turns that capacity into sales.

## What partnership could look like

This isn't a fundraising ask — it's an open door for the right collaborators:

- **Production capacity partners** — CMT houses that can absorb volume alongside Tailorcloth as
  the shirt program scales past what's currently committed.
- **Retail or pop-up hosting partners** — boutiques, formalwear rental shops, and church
  bookstores positioned for a consignment or pop-up relationship.
- **Referral and community partners** — photographers, wedding venues, and ministry leaders
  positioned to introduce customers directly.

## Roadmap snapshot

- **Q4 2026** — technical foundation (SEO, content, first blog posts), community partnerships
  launched, first trunk shows and referral program live, push toward 150 shirts / 75 suits.
- **2027** — repeat the Q4 pace for all four quarters; add paid acquisition only once the organic
  channels have proven the message; resolve production capacity ahead of the shirt-volume ramp.
