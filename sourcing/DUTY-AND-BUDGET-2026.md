## 1. What US import duty actually looks like right now

The 2025 tariff landscape everyone remembers is gone. What happened, in order:

1. **2026-02-20 — Supreme Court kills the IEEPA tariffs.** *Learning Resources v. Trump*,
   6-3: IEEPA does not authorize tariffs. All the 2025 "reciprocal" and fentanyl tariffs
   terminated; CBP stopped collecting 2026-02-24. **VERIFIED** (slip opinion, CRS, multiple
   firm alerts, cross-checked by the refutation pass).
2. **2026-02-24 — Section 122 surcharge replaces them, at 10%.** A 15% raise was announced
   on social media but **never legally implemented** — importers paid 10% the whole time.
   (A source claiming 15% was refuted 3-0 in verification.) **VERIFIED**.
3. **2026-07-24 — Section 122 expires by operation of law** (150-day statutory cap; Congress
   did not extend). Replaced the same minute by **new Section 301 duties of 10-12.5%** by
   country tier on ~60-80 economies, with no sunset. Brazil carries an **additional 25%**
   Section 301. **VERIFIED**.
4. **China Section 301 List 3 never moved:** woven fabrics carry **+25%** (since 2018),
   unaffected by all of the above. Hong Kong transshipment does not avoid it. **VERIFIED**.
5. **De minimis is dead and staying dead.** The $800 duty-free parcel exemption has been
   suspended for all countries since 2025-08-29 (EO 14324), was continued independently of
   the IEEPA ruling (EO 14388), codified in CBP regulations 2026-06-24, upheld by the CIT
   2026-08-13, and is repealed by statute effective 2027-07-01. **Every parcel, even a $50
   cut length, needs a formal or informal customs entry with a 10-digit HTS code.**
   **VERIFIED** (Federal Register + CIT ruling).

Small-parcel kicker: on a $70 parcel, the entry/brokerage fee often exceeds the duty.
Consolidate international orders, or buy domestic.

## 4. What this changes in the plan

- **The $15-30/yd gap now has real answers**, and the two fastest are domestic: Fabric Mart
  and B. Black's wool sale need no outreach at all, just orders. Metro's codes third.
- **Mexico is the strategic play**: CIMEXLANA is the single best match to the original brief
  found to date (integrated, budget-priced, courts tailors, duty-free, 3-day ground shipping
  distance from Louisiana). Novalan backs it up. Both go to the top of the outreach queue.
- **Kill the assumptions**: Turkey is not duty-free and never was; Morocco's FTA has no
  suiting/shirting mills behind it; Brazil is priced out by tariff; China's 1m-MOQ miracle
  is real ex-works but ~50% duty + per-parcel entry fees eat it.
- **Peru is the shirting sleeper**: Creditex pima at Lima retail prices with a 0% duty lane
  would undercut everything but closeouts — if they'll ship small.
