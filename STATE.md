**This is a different question from "125 shipped".** That figure measures rows this pipeline has
generated AND graded AND published — 7.2%. It is the right number for *our* quality bar and the
wrong number for *the storefront*, because 1,102 rows already show a photograph; most were generated
before this project's QC gate existed and are the 859 carried as `legacy-shipped-unverified`.

| | rows | what it needs |
|---|---:|---|
| showing a drawing | **645** | a photograph — generation, the expensive half |
| showing an unverified photo | ~977 | grading only — **free**, and it may well approve most of them |
| verified and shipped | 125 | done |

because two thirds of the catalog already has a picture and simply has not been audited.

`/images/generated/` path and is therefore known to be a photograph, the classifier misreads 4 —
a 0.4% false-DRAWING rate. All four are one honest edge case: pale ecru pocketing on a light-grey
sweep (`coin-none`, `coin-left`) is genuinely mostly-white and nearly colourless. So the 645 carries
a handful of pale photographs; it is a close estimate, not an exact count.
