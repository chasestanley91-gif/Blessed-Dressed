## 5. BLOCKING SPEND — options whose "blueprint" is not a drawing

Measured from the pixels by `tools/blueprint_triage.mjs --all`, not from filenames.
These files are catalogued as tech packs but are fabric-texture crops, icons or
essentially blank frames. They cannot serve as a QC yardstick: QC scores fidelity
*to the drawing*, so generating against one of these scores a render of a fabric
swatch and can still pass.

  **~135 credits** that would have bought worthless images.

field, rule one of: (a) source the genuine supplier tech pack, (b) re-point the row
at a correct existing drawing (`tools/repoint_supplier_blueprints.mjs`), or
(c) accept label-only grading and mark the field QC-exempt.
