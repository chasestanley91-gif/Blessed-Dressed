## The finding that should change what happens next

**Corrections on this pipeline do not converge — they oscillate.**

Five options were re-shot carrying an explicit `KEEP EXACTLY AS-IS` block naming 12–18 individually
measured features apiece, with every grader told that a locked-feature regression is at least a major
finding. **Four of the five lost locked features anyway.**

The clearest case: **`pleat-no-single-dart` went backwards.** It entered at **min 93, zero critical and
zero major** — the closest option in the entire queue to shipping — and returned at **min 88 with three
majors**, having lost the belt-loop pitch ratio that the whole grading normalises against.

`jeans-square` shows the mechanism numerically: its vertical drop was **18–26% short**, and after
correction is **46–79% long**. The corner turn flipped from 5.0° too open to 5.3–8.1° too closed. It
hit its target and sailed past it.

### What follows

1. **A third attempt is not reliably better than the second.** Compare the current verdict against the
   previous one before spending. If an option already has zero criticals, another attempt is about as
   likely to cost ground as gain it.
2. **Prefer waiving to re-shooting** once an option is at zero critical/major and within a few points.
   `PASS_WAIVED` exists for this and is under-used relative to the risk.
3. **Keep the keep-as-is block** — as a *detector*. It caught all four regressions, which is the only
   reason they are visible rather than shipped unnoticed. It is not a *preventer*.

## Pipeline hazard worth knowing

**The credit is spent at `generate_image`; the artifact only becomes durable at `record_generation`.**
Between those two steps the result exists solely on an expiring CloudFront URL. An interruption there
silently destroys paid work — it happened this session when plan mode activated mid-run, and three
images survived only because the subagents reported their `jobId` and `rawUrl` instead of retrying.

Any generation agent must run step 9 immediately after step 8, and must report the job id on failure
rather than regenerating.

## Corrections made to my own earlier claims this session

Recorded so the reasoning stays auditable:

- **`coin-both`** — I graded it by hand as min 82 / fault fixed. An independent grader returned min 45 /
  fault **not** fixed, and is right: I measured the U by its **bounding box** (1.65:1) where a proper
  rotated-frame fit gives **2.85:1** against a drawn 1.21:1. The fault was two-part — sign *and*
  magnitude — and only the sign was corrected. Measured as error magnitude the "fix" is **worse** than
  what it replaced.
- **`lp-slanted-flap-55`** — filed as saturated on a depth ratio; the actual defect is the wrong
  construction entirely. *Measure the discriminator the label names before the dimension it states.*
- **The founding `lapel-notch-68` lesson** — the oft-cited "39.8° contradicts the 68° label" was itself
  mis-measured. 39.8° is the **gorge seam inclination**; the true notch opening is 76.7°, and the labels
  are correct at a constant +8.8° chord offset. The *lesson* survives; the specific claim was withdrawn.
