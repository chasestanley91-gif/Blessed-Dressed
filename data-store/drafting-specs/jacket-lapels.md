# Jacket Lapels — Drafting Specification

_Authored by the brand owner, 2026-08-01. Verbatim. This document is LAW for the options it names; illustrations and other descriptions aid, not direct._

---

What you're asking for is not a style description but a drafting specification. I'll describe the lapels as if I were handing instructions to a bespoke cutter and patternmaker. These descriptions assume a standard men's jacket block with:

Neck Point (NP)
Shoulder Point (SP)
Break Point (BP)
Gorge Point (GP)
Center Front (CF)
Chest Line (CL)
Waist Line (WL)

and lapel widths measured perpendicular to the roll line.

## NOTCH 45°
`lapel-notch-45`

### Geometric Character

Extremely relaxed notch.

The notch opening forms approximately:

    45°

The notch resembles a shallow V cut.

### Draft Shape

The collar edge descends gently into the gorge.

The upper notch edge and lower lapel edge are nearly equal in angle.

          /
         /
    ----/
       /
      /

### Visual Appearance

The notch does not aggressively separate collar and lapel.

Everything feels fluid.

### Sewing Interpretation

Minimal notch excavation.

The gorge cut remains shallow.

The notch point remains rounded rather than sharp.

### Style DNA
Italian
Soft tailoring
Relaxed luxury

## NOTCH 50°
`lapel-notch-50`

### Geometry

Slightly sharper than 45°.

    50°

Creates a more visible separation.

### Draft Characteristics

Increase notch depth approximately:

    5mm-8mm

compared to 45°.

### Appearance

The lapel now appears intentional rather than soft.

### Visual Effect

More chest definition.

Greater structure.

## NOTCH 55°
`lapel-notch-55`

### Geometry

Classic benchmark notch.

    55°

### Draft Construction

    7-9cm
    below shoulder seam

Notch depth:

    2cm-3cm

### Visual Appearance

Perfect balance.

Neither aggressive nor soft.

### Optical Effect

✅ Balanced shoulders

✅ Balanced chest

✅ Timeless tailoring
