# Shirt Collars — Drafting Specification

_Authored by the brand owner, 2026-08-01. Verbatim. This document is LAW for the options it names; illustrations and other descriptions aid, not direct._

---

For shirt collars, the collar is the architectural frame of the face. More than any other shirt component, the collar controls facial proportions, neck proportions, tie presentation, and how the shirt interacts with a jacket.

A collar is composed of two primary structures:

    1. Collar Stand
    2. Collar Leaf (Visible Collar)

The stand determines elevation.

The leaf determines character.

For drafting purposes:

    NP = Neck Point
    CF = Center Front
    CB = Center Back

    L = Point Length
    S = Spread Angle
    SH = Stand Height
    FH = Fall Height

## LONG POINT COLLAR 8.5cm

### Geometry

    Point Length = 8.5cm

    Spread = 50°-60°

    Tip Angle = 20°-30°

### Draft Shape

         /\
        /  \
       /    \
      /      \

Each collar point extends deeply below the jacket lapel line.

The side edge and outer edge converge gradually toward a sharp tip.

### Physical Appearance

The collar appears to pour downward from the neck.

The points disappear beneath the lapels.

### Visual Illusion

✅ Longer neck

✅ Narrower face

✅ Taller appearance

✅ Smaller knot appearance

### Sewing Interpretation

Maintain a very gradual taper.

Avoid shortening the point curve.

Tip should remain extremely sharp.

### Mental Picture

Two cavalry sabers descending from beneath the jaw.

For the Blessed&Dressed system, the biggest visual transformations come from the interaction of:

    Collar Type
    +
    Point Length
    +
    Spread Angle
    +
    Stand Height
    +
    Interlining Firmness

Those five variables determine roughly 90% of what the customer perceives as the collar's character, authority, elegance, and facial framing effect.
