SUBJECT
Craft option: Inner Cuff (bias cutting). The inner cuff facing is cut at exactly 45 degrees to the fabric grain, so its blue stripes run diagonally, while every other visible part — outer cuff, sleeve, placket — keeps straight-grain stripes; never diagonal on the outer face, never straight on the inner facing. A single row of edge topstitching runs 6 mm inside every cuff edge, visible on the outer face and echoed on the inner facing. The outer cuff face is ONE uninterrupted panel of fabric; its only seams are the sleeve attachment seam and the perimeter topstitching. Ratio anchors: cuff depth about six button diameters (6.5 cm to a 10.5 mm button); topstitch offset about half a button diameter; bias stripes cross the straight stripes at exactly 45 degrees; the bias facing fills the FULL inner depth of the cuff. Distinction from siblings: in Inner Collar Stand and Inner Top Collar the diagonal grain sits at the collar — here the collar is out of frame and the ONLY diagonal-stripe component in the image is the inner cuff facing; in None (Standard Grain) no diagonal exists anywhere.

FORBIDDEN
